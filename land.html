Four checks, one scan

Everything a manual label audit covers, run automatically the moment a photo is uploaded.

01

AI-powered label scanning

Capture a package with a phone camera or upload an existing photo. Computer vision locates the principal display panel and isolates every printed declaration on it.

02

Mandatory declaration detection

OCR reads and classifies each field against the declarations Rule 6 requires — manufacturer details, common name, net quantity, packing date, MRP, and consumer care information.

03

Font size & readability check

Measures the printed height of declared text against the minimum letter and numeral sizes Rule 8 sets for the package's net quantity, and flags anything under-sized.

04

Automated compliance reports

Every scan produces a report listing what passed, what failed, and the exact rule each violation falls under — ready to export or hand to a supplier.
